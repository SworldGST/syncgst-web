Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

1. Strict Non-Refundable Policy

SyncGST provides digital software tools and intellectual property that are instantly accessible and applicable upon purchase. Due to the nature of our digital services, all payments and subscription purchases made on SyncGST are final and strictly non-refundable.

We do not provide refunds, credits, or prorated billing adjustments for any partially used subscription periods, accidental purchases, or failure to utilize the software within your subscription timeframe.

2. Cancellations

You may choose to stop using our services at any time. If you decide not to renew your subscription, your account will simply revert to the free tier upon the expiration of your current active license period. No further charges will be applied, but no refunds will be issued for the time remaining.

3. Exceptional Circumstances

The only exception to our non-refundable policy is in the event of a documented technical error caused by our payment gateway (e.g., your card being charged twice for a single transaction). In such rare technical anomalies, the duplicate charge will be fully refunded to the original payment method.

4. Pre-Purchase Verification

We encourage all users to thoroughly review our free tools, features, and system requirements before committing to a premium subscription plan. By finalizing your payment through Razorpay, you acknowledge and agree to this non-refundable policy.

5. Support

If you experience any issues accessing your purchased client slots or deploying the software, please contact our support team immediately so we can assist you with technical troubleshooting.
