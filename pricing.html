Frequently Asked Questions

How do I purchase a plan?

Simply sign up for a free account. Inside your dashboard, navigate to the "Licenses & Billing" tab, select your desired tier, and pay securely via Razorpay to instantly unlock your slots and premium tools.

Are the premium tools included for free?

Access to specific premium tools (JSON Cleaner, Bank Injector, Cash Sales Generator) depends on your active subscription tier. Upgrading your plan automatically unlocks the corresponding tools in your dashboard.

Can I change my registered clients later?

No. Once a client GSTIN is locked into a slot, it is permanently registered to your account to prevent license sharing. You will need to upgrade to a higher tier to manage additional clients.
