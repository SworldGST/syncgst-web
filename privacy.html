Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

1. Information Security & Local Processing

At SyncGST, we respect the strict confidentiality of your financial records. We do not read, process, or store your clients' accounting data or compliance records on our cloud servers.

Our proprietary extraction utilities and processing engines are designed to format data securely and transmit it through our localized bridging client. All core financial data processing and routing occur within your localized environment, ensuring records remain exclusively on your authorized hardware.

2. Information We Collect

To provide and manage our service, we collect the following basic account information:

  • Account Data: Your email address and securely hashed password.
  • License Data: The GSTIN numbers and Party Names of the clients you register in your dashboard to manage your quota.
  • Payment Data: Billing records of your subscription purchases (processed securely by Razorpay). We do not store your credit card or bank details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your login and secure your account via OTPs.
  • Validate your active GSTIN licenses when you use our premium injection tools.
  • Send you transaction receipts and important software updates.

4. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party providers, such as Razorpay, for secure payment processing. These providers have their own privacy policies governing the data they collect during the checkout process.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to our support team via the Contact page.
